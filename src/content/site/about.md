## Our History

Keene Disc Golf Club is a loose organization of like-minded locals who wanted to bring a disc golf course to the Keene area. After years of planning and thousands of donated volunteer hours, we built [Otter Brook Disc Golf Course](/courses/otter-brook/).

## Our Mission

Our focus is on:

- Maintaining Otter Brook and The Pines at Wheelock
- Promoting disc golf in the Monadnock Region and New Hampshire
- Bringing regional tournaments to the area
- Organizing local leagues and supporting the local disc golf community
- Introducing disc golf and Otter Brook to nearby communities

## Membership

Member dues help fund the course. Members receive an official bag tag to both Otter Brook and The Pines at Wheelock, access to the Club Championship, and a voice in the club. [Become a member](/membership/) to join.
