## Course Overview

The Pines at Wheelock is a short, flat, 9-hole par 27 course in Keene's Wheelock Park. It has turf tees and is free to play, which makes it a good place for new players to start.

## Good to Know

- **Length:** about 1,466 feet in total
- **Cost:** free
- **Shared space:** this is a mixed-use public park, so watch for other park users
- **GPS:** 42.941251, -72.298832
