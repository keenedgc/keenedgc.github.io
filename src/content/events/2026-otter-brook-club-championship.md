The final Tag round of the year, followed by lunch provided by the club. This event is **open to Keene Disc Golf Club members only**.

- **Check-in:** 8:30-9:15 AM at the Pavilion. Bring your Otter Brook tag.
- **Players meeting:** 9:15-9:30 AM, then walk to starting tees
- **Round:** 9:30 AM-12:30 PM, singles with a shotgun start on the Mystery Otter Brook Jumbo Extended Layout
- **Lunch and awards:** 12:30-3:00 PM
- **Entry:** $10
- **Registration deadline:** November 7 at 8:00 PM
