The Keene Disc Golf Club runs multiple leagues. Times and season dates may have changed, so check [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/KeeneDiscGolfClub) before heading out.

## Sunday Tags (Summer)

- **Course:** Otter Brook
- **Season:** April through October
- **Time:** 9:00 AM
- **Format:** Singles, except the last Sunday of the month, which is doubles
- **Buy-in:** $5

## Sunday Dubz (Winter)

- **Course:** Otter Brook
- **Season:** November through March
- **Time:** 9:30 AM
- **Format:** Doubles
- **Meet:** Top lot at Otter Brook
- **Buy-in:** $5

## Wheelock Wednesday

- **Course:** The Pines at Wheelock
- **Season:** April through September
- **Time:** ~6:00 PM
- **Format:** Singles on even weeks. Special formats on odd weeks.
- **Meet:** At Hole 1
- **Buy-in:** $5

## Where the Buy-in Goes

Each $5 buy-in sends $1 to the club, $1 to the Ace Pot, and $3 to player payouts.
