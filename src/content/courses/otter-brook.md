## Course Overview

Otter Brook is an 18-hole wooded course on US Army Corps of Engineers land just outside Keene, built with their permission after years of planning and thousands of donated volunteer hours from Keene Disc Golf Club members.

![Aerial map of the 18 holes at Otter Brook, showing the upper and lower parking lots](/images/uploads/otter-brook-course-map.jpg)

## Getting There

The course is on Route 9 East, 2.8 miles past the Washington St exit in Keene. Coming from Concord, it is 2.7 miles past the Sullivan Country Store.

- **GPS:** 42.968250, -72.227833
- **From Route 101:** stay on Route 101 to Route 9 rather than following GPS onto back roads.
- **Otter Brook Dam:** do not follow signs for the dam. There are no baskets there.

## Parking and Where to Start

**In season (Memorial Day weekend to Labor Day weekend):** park in the recreation area during its regular hours and leave before it closes. Play typically starts at Hole 1.

**Off season and after hours:** park in the top lot and walk in. Play typically starts at Hole 5, down the road to the right, past the bridge.

![Stone and log steps leading into the woods at the entrance to Hole 1](/images/uploads/otter-brook-hole-1-entrance.jpg)

## Amenities

In season, Otter Brook Beach has toilets, picnic tables, and grills that players may use. Please respect other park users.

![The sandy shoreline at Otter Brook Beach](/images/uploads/otter-brook-beach.jpg)

## Course Etiquette

There are no trash cans on the course, so carry out everything you carry in.
